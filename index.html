Help
How Stub works
Your bank statement shows the total. Stub shows what you actually bought.
Scan, upload, or add manually
Take a photo or pick one from your library — it reads items and prices right on your phone. No photo? Add one by hand instead.
Fix anything it gets wrong
Store name, items, prices, categories, notes — everything's editable, right on the receipt.
Search, sort & filter
Find receipts by store or item, sort by date or amount, filter to one category, or just business expenses.
See where your money goes
Summary breaks spending down by category and by month, with charts, updated automatically as you add receipts.
Set category budgets
Give any category a monthly target and track it against what you've actually spent.
Track return windows
Set a return-by date on anything, and see what's still returnable before the deadline passes.
Tag business expenses
Flag purchases as business expenses, filter to just those, and they're broken out in your CSV export.
Share or back up
Share any single receipt from your phone, or export everything as a backup (.json) or spreadsheet (.csv).
